title: Tracks
description: Several objectives for one piece of code, on one trunk.
---

<Note>Tracks are experimental.</Note>

A **track** is one objective of a campaign with several, such as the accuracy of a model and its cost to run. Each
track has its own primary metric. A keep on one track must hold the primary of every other track within its margin.

`piloop init --example titanic-tracks` makes a campaign with two tracks on Kaggle's Titanic data. Its evaluate command
prints `accuracy`, on a fixed holdout, and `cost_seconds`, the CPU time to train and predict:

```toml
[[metrics]]
name = "accuracy"
direction = "higher"
role = "primary"
track = "accuracy"
margin = 0.02          # what a cost keep may give up for each keep
floor = 0.74           # and never below this

[[metrics]]
name = "cost_seconds"
direction = "lower"
role = "primary"
track = "cost"
margin = 0.3           # what an accuracy keep may add for each keep
floor = 2.0            # and never past this

[tracks.accuracy]
share = 0.75           # of the implementer slots
addendum = "briefs/accuracy.md"

[tracks.cost]
share = 0.25
addendum = "briefs/cost.md"
```

## How tracks work

- Each primary metric names its `track`, and needs a `margin`.
- `share` is the part of the implementer slots that the track gets. The shares sum to 1, and a share of 0 stops a
  track. `addendum` is a file that piloop adds to the brief of the track's runs.
- Every idea names its track: `piloop idea add titanic-tracks "Add a family-size feature" --track accuracy`.
- A candidate is judged on the track of its idea. The primaries of the other tracks are its guardrails, each with its
  own margin and floor.
- All tracks share one head. A keep on any track moves it.
- A free slot goes to the track that is furthest below its share and has a ready idea.

A campaign with one primary metric has one track, and needs no `[tracks]` table. `[stop] target` is not allowed with
several tracks.

## What you see

`piloop status` and `piloop watch` show one line for each track: its primary at the head and at the start, the
distance to its floor, its keeps, and its running runs against its share. A verdict reason names the other track's
primary when it decides:

```text
guardrails: cost_seconds 0.49 vs 0.09 (+0.4; …) beyond its margin 0.3
```
